# CHANGELOG — FYONA ERP

## [1.0.0] — 2026-07-11
### Added
- Complete ERP system with 19 modules
- Authentication with JWT, MFA interfaces, session management
- Customer, Supplier, Product management
- Sales and Purchase invoicing with tax/discount
- Inventory tracking with stock movements
- Accounting: Chart of Accounts, Journal, Trial Balance, BS, P&L
- Banking, Expenses, Payments, Cheques management
- HR: Employees, Contracts, Leaves, Payroll
- CRM: Leads management
- Projects & Tasks
- Reports center
- In-app notifications
- Financial and HR dashboards
- Dark/Light theme, RTL support
- Arabic/English localization
- Material 3 design system
- 14 reusable shared widgets
- Clean Architecture with Feature First
- 49 automated tests

### Technical
- Flutter 3.44.4 / Dart 3.12.2
- State Management: Riverpod
- Navigation: GoRouter
- Networking: Dio with interceptors
- Storage: FlutterSecureStorage
- Charts: fl_chart
